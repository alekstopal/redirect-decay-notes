Notes on a 301 after a drop: why the lift fades

When a redirected drop stops pushing rankings after a few weeks, the usual cause is that Google has re-evaluated the old domain as a new topic and no longer passes its relevance to the target. The links stay in the index, but the signal weakens.

My order of work: check what the drop used to rank for, compare it with the money page, and only redirect when the topics overlap. If they do not, rebuild the old content on the drop and link from it instead of redirecting.
